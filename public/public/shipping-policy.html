Shipping policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Delivery coverage and estimate

UDECS offers India-wide shipping, subject to serviceability and product availability for your delivery PIN. Delivery is estimated within 5-7 days after dispatch. It is not a guaranteed delivery date for every PIN; any product-specific schedule will be confirmed before payment or dispatch.

Shipping charges

Shipping availability and charges are confirmed before dispatch. Review your order's final charges before paying. For quotation products, goods and GST totals do not include shipping until a shipping quote is confirmed.

Order queries

Use your order reference when contacting UDECS about dispatch, a delay, damage or non-delivery. Do not rely on a marketing post as proof that stock is reserved or that a dispatch date has been confirmed.
