Payment policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Available payment methods

PayU online payment and direct UPI QR are available for eligible checkout orders. COD is temporarily paused for new orders. Existing orders are unchanged. Payment options shown in checkout apply to your order; do not assume every PayU method is available for every transaction.

Prices and charges

Retail product prices include GST. B2B or wholesale quotations may show GST separately where stated. Review the displayed goods, tax, shipping and other charges before paying. Any PayU shopping charge is shown separately in checkout. Shipping-quote orders require availability and shipping confirmation before payment.

Payment and order confirmation

Saving an order does not mean payment succeeded. PayU payment status must be confirmed. Direct UPI QR payments remain subject to verification against the order reference. Do not pay a different amount or destination from the instructions confirmed for your order.
