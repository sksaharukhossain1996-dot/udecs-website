Return and refund policy

Effective 8 October 2026

7-day return for unused products

For retail orders, you may request a return within 7 days of delivery if the product has not been used. Contact UDECS with your order reference and product details so the correct return instructions can be provided. Do not send a parcel to a supplier or dispatch warehouse without confirmed return instructions.

B2B wholesale orders

After dispatch, B2B wholesale orders have no voluntary return or refund. Before dispatch, paid B2B orders may be cancelled with a refund in 5-7 days. The 7-day unused-product retail return offer does not apply to B2B wholesale orders. Statutory rights and remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded remain unaffected.

Problems with an order

If a product is defective, damaged, wrong or not as described, contact UDECS with the order reference and details of the problem. The unused-product condition does not remove any remedy available under applicable consumer law. Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected by this policy.

Refund timeframe

For an eligible return, the refund timeframe is 5-7 days after UDECS receives the returned parcel. The returned product is checked during this process. Contact UDECS with your order reference if the refund has not been received within that period. Contact UDECS to confirm the refund route for your payment method.

Return shipping

UDECS pays the return courier charge for returns under this policy. Contact UDECS for the return instructions before sending the product, so the return shipment can be arranged correctly.
