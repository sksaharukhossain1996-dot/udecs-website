UDYAM registration

MSME/UDYAM registration is completely free at udyamregistration.gov.in. Government rules do not authorize any private agency, so UDECS does not sell this service. Beware of anyone charging for it.

Barcode (GS1)

GS1 India does not authorize any agent or facilitator - get barcodes directly at gs1india.org. GS1 pricing runs high (about ₹50,000 for 100 barcodes, 1 year, small business), so apply only if you truly need retail barcodes.

GeM seller registration

Government e-Marketplace seller registration is completely free at gem.gov.in. Government advisories warn against anyone charging for it, so UDECS does not sell this service.
